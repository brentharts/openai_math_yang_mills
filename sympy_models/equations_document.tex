\begin{abstract}
\noindent
This document collects equations from selected manuscripts of the OpenAI mathematics release
and from companion references, grouped by the entry number of \texttt{CONTENTS.md}. Each equation
is typeset from a sympy model, and the model's checks have passed: they test that the stated
formulas are consistent with one another and, where noted, against independent computation.
They do \emph{not} re-prove any theorem. This is fork-level material, not an official OpenAI document.
\end{abstract}

\section*{Provenance}
Every equation carries a source kind:
\begin{description}
  \item[\textmd{\textsc{paper}}] transcribed from a manuscript in \texttt{preprints/}, with the theorem or
        equation number given in the source line;
  \item[\textmd{\textsc{repo-doc}}] quoted from this fork's markdown notes and not re-read from the paper, so
        check the paper before citing it;
  \item[\textmd{\textsc{textbook}}] standard background that is not in any manuscript here;
  \item[\textmd{\textsc{derived}}] obtained by sympy from other registered equations, including the
        compositions generated by the knowledge graph;
  \item[\textmd{\textsc{knowledge-base}}] quoted from the library copied from RosettaMath
        (\texttt{knowledgebase.py}) and cross-checked here against a model of the manuscripts. It is a second
        transcription of a result, not a reading of the paper.
\end{description}
A theorem statement whose \textsc{paper} entry lists no checks is recorded verbatim without a
computation attached. Notes under an equation state what it does not say.
