\begin{abstract}
\noindent
We join equations from the OpenAI math release and its companion repositories by reading each letter as the
quantity it denotes, solving for that quantity and checking the result in sympy (\nChecks{} checks pass in the build).
Three threads follow. (i)~The hat monotile's inflation factor is $\varphi^4$, and so is the ratio of unreflected to
reflected hats: the first follows from the characteristic polynomial $(x^2-1)(x^2-7x+1)$ of the four-metatile
substitution, whose spectrum is $\{1,-1,\varphi^4,\varphi^{-4}\}$; the second because the unreflected-hat counts are the
$H$ row of that matrix. The Spectre's inflation $4+\sqrt{15}$ is not a power of $\varphi$, and only its perimeter growth
$2+\sqrt5=\varphi^3$ is golden. (ii)~For Yang--Mills we do not claim a mass gap, which the release does not contain;
we state the chain a gap must satisfy, a coupling-independent $E_{\mathrm{gap}}=c\,\hbar c\Lambda_L$ with
$c=\lim m_L/(a\Lambda_L)$, check its consistency with two-loop scaling, and keep the analogy with $O(N)$ lattice
models a correspondence, never an identity. (iii)~The $E_8$ decompositions behind the chirality theorem are rebuilt from
the Cartan matrix and tied to the Standard Model's $16$, to $\sin^2\theta_W=3/8$ and to a weight count that does not
admit three generations in $128$ weights. This is fork-level material, not an official OpenAI document.
\end{abstract}
