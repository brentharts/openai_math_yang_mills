\begin{abstract}
\noindent
Equations that state one quantity can be set equal once each is solved for it, provided every letter has first
been read as the quantity it denotes. We build a knowledge graph of \nEq{} equations and \nQty{} quantities in the
manner of RosettaMath~\cite{rosettamath}, extended with models of the OpenAI math release, of the Spectre and
Chair44 tilings, and of the chirality--$E_8$ repository, and draw its rows as large displays. Every term of every
row is substituted back into its source equation and every number is checked in sympy: \nChecks{} checks over
\nRegEq{} registered equations pass in the build. Correspondences between different theories, such as the $O(N)$
model and Yang--Mills, or the chirality theorem's family $3$ and Lisi's triality $3$, are drawn as arrows and never
as equalities. \nExplore{} exploration scripts then test the connections the graph suggests: the coincidences
of $\mathfrak{su}(N)$ with $\mathfrak{so}(n)$, the orthogonal-plus-spinor pattern of the exceptional algebras, the
gradings of $E_8$, how many generations a weight-vector count allows, and the weak mixing angle run from $3/8$ to
the $Z$ mass. This is fork-level material, not an official OpenAI document.
\end{abstract}
