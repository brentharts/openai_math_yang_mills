\begin{abstract}
\noindent
Equations that state one quantity are set equal after each is solved for it, and a row of such
equalities is drawn as a single large display, in the manner of RosettaMath~\cite{rosettamath}.
The rows are generated from a knowledge graph and checked in sympy: every term, substituted back
into its source equation, gives an identity, and rows of numbers are checked equal. The companion
document \texttt{equations\_document} gives the full equations, their sources and their checks.
This is fork-level material, not an official OpenAI document.
\end{abstract}
